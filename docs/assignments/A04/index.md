## Feature 1

Feature One serves as the base to which the motor will be bolted. For this design, the cross-sectional area of the feature must be determined. I selected a length of 25 mm, which is greater than the motor’s 22 mm diameter, and a height of 30 mm. This left the width, b, as the remaining variable to be determined based on both stress and stiffness requirements.

I selected PLA as the material and obtained its modulus of elasticity and yield strength from MatWeb. A safety factor of 3 was provided for the design. Using the formulas provided during lecture, I determined that the minimum required width, b, was 3.32 mm. This value was obtained by rearranging the maximum stress equation to solve for b. The selected dimension was chosen because it satisfies both the maximum allowable stress and maximum deflection requirements. Due to this being a minimum value, I chose to go with 5mm to not only satisfy the equations but also make the math simpler.
